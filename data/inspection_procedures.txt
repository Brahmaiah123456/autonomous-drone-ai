INSPECTION PROCEDURES

Document ID: IP-001
Version: 1.0

1. SOLAR PANEL INSPECTION

The objective of a solar panel inspection is to identify visible or thermal abnormalities.

The drone may capture RGB and thermal images during inspection.

Possible abnormalities include:
- Cracked panels
- Broken panels
- Discoloration
- Hotspots
- Missing components
- Unusual thermal patterns

2. ANOMALY CONFIDENCE

The AI inspection system assigns a confidence score to detected anomalies.

If anomaly confidence is below 70%, the result should be treated as uncertain and sent for human review.

If anomaly confidence is between 70% and 80%, additional evidence should be collected before making a decision.

If anomaly confidence is above 80%, a secondary inspection may be recommended.

3. SECONDARY INSPECTION

A secondary inspection provides additional evidence about a suspected anomaly.

Before starting a secondary inspection, verify:

- Battery level is at least 30%
- GPS signal is available
- Weather conditions are safe
- The mission location is permitted
- No critical drone fault is present

4. THERMAL ANOMALY

A thermal anomaly may indicate a possible equipment problem.

The AI must not automatically conclude that equipment is damaged based only on a single image.

The system should:
1. Identify the possible anomaly.
2. Retrieve the relevant inspection procedure.
3. Check the confidence score.
4. Request additional evidence when necessary.
5. Recommend a secondary inspection when appropriate.

5. HUMAN REVIEW

Human review is required when:
- Confidence is below 70%
- Evidence is contradictory
- Safety information is unavailable
- A high-risk action is recommended
- The system cannot find a relevant approved procedure

6. INSPECTION REPORT

The final inspection report should contain:

- Mission ID
- Inspection type
- Location
- Detected anomaly
- Confidence score
- Evidence used
- Retrieved procedure
- Risk level
- Recommended action
- Human approval status

7. DECISION PRINCIPLE

The system should prefer safe and explainable decisions.

The AI should provide the evidence supporting its recommendation.

If the required information cannot be retrieved from approved documents, the system should not invent information and should request human review.
