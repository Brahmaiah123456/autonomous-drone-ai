DRONE SAFETY POLICY

Document ID: DSP-001
Version: 1.0

1. BATTERY SAFETY

A drone must not begin a new mission if the battery level is below 30%.

If the battery level falls below 20% during a mission, the drone must immediately initiate Return-to-Home (RTH).

If the battery level falls below 10%, the mission must be aborted and the drone must perform an emergency landing when safe.

2. WEATHER SAFETY

A mission must not start when weather conditions are unsafe.

The maximum permitted wind speed for normal operations is 30 km/h.

If wind speed exceeds 30 km/h during a mission, the mission should be paused or the drone should return to a safe location.

Drone operations must not continue during severe weather conditions such as thunderstorms.

3. GPS SAFETY

The drone requires a stable GPS signal for autonomous outdoor missions.

If GPS signal is lost for more than 10 seconds, autonomous mission execution must be paused.

The drone should attempt to restore GPS connectivity or initiate Return-to-Home.

4. HUMAN APPROVAL

Human approval is required before executing a high-risk mission action.

High-risk actions include:
- Flying into restricted areas
- Continuing a mission with critical battery levels
- Continuing operations during unsafe weather
- Performing actions when the system confidence is below 70%

5. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

If a critical safety condition is detected, the drone must stop the planned mission action.

The system should prioritize:
1. Human safety
2. Drone safety
3. Returning to a safe location
4. Completing the mission

6. ANOMALY DETECTION

If an inspection system detects a possible infrastructure anomaly with confidence above 80%, a secondary inspection may be recommended.

A secondary inspection must still pass all battery, weather, GPS and safety checks.

7. DECISION POLICY

The AI system must not make safety-critical decisions using generated knowledge alone.

Safety decisions must be supported by the approved safety documentation.

When required information cannot be found, the system must request human review rather than inventing a rule.
